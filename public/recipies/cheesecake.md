# San Sebastian Protein Pro MAX Cheesecake

A rich, Basque-style burnt cheesecake adapted with paneer [BECAUSE MASCARPONE IS FUCKING EXPENSIVE IN INDIA] for a healthy and affordable alt.

---

## Tentative Macros Breakdown

_(Based on 8 generous servings, calculated with low-fat/lean options & zero-calorie sweetener where applicable)_

| Metric       | Total Batch | Per Serving (1/8th) |
| ------------ | ----------- | ------------------- |
| **Calories** | ~2,180 kcal | **~272 kcal**       |
| **Protein**  | ~142 g      | **~18 g**           |
| **Carbs**    | ~55 g       | **~7 g**            |
| **Fats**     | ~152 g      | **~19 g**           |

_(Note: Adding the **Protein Chocolate Sauce** adds **~165 kcal, 26g Protein, 4g Carbs, 5g Fat** to the total batch—adding roughly **+5-10g Protein** per slice!)_

---

## Ingredients

### Cheesecake Base

- **Paneer (Soft / Malai):** 500 g
- **Whole Eggs:** 5 large
- **Thick Hung Curd / Greek Yogurt:** 200 g
- **Fresh Cream / Low-Fat Heavy Cream:** 200 ml
- **Sugar / Zero-Calorie Sweetener (Erythritol/Stevia):** 1 cup to 1¼ cups _(to taste)_
- **Cornstarch / Cornflour:** 3 tbsp
- **Vanilla Extract:** 1 tbsp
- **Lemon Juice:** 2 tsp
- **Salt:** ¼ tsp

### Optional High-Protein Chocolate Pour-Over Sauce

- **Chocolate Whey Protein Powder:** 1 scoop (~30 g)
- **Low-Fat Fresh Cream or Greek Yogurt:** 2–3 tbsp _(for creaminess)_
- **Unsweetened Cocoa Powder:** 1 tbsp _(for deep chocolate flavor)_
- **Milk / Almond Milk:** 3–5 tbsp _(splash by splash to reach pouring consistency)_
- **Zero-Calorie Sweetener / Stevia:** To taste _(optional)_

## Equipment Needed

- **Pan:** 8-inch round springform or cake pan _(a 6-inch pan will overflow with a 500 g batch)_.
- **Blender:** High-power blender jug _(if under 1.5L capacity, blend in two batches)_.
- **Parchment Paper:** 2 large overlapping sheets, crinkled.

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prep Oven & Pan

1. Preheat your oven to **220°C (425°F)**.
2. Line the 8-inch springform or cake pan with two overlapping sheets of crinkled parchment paper.
3. Ensure the parchment paper overhangs the edges by at least **2 inches** to accommodate the rise during baking.

### 2. Blend the Base

1. In a high-power blender, add the **paneer (500 g)**, **hung curd (200 g)**, **cream (200 ml)**, **sweetener (1 to 1¼ cups)**, **cornstarch (3 tbsp)**, **vanilla extract (1 tbsp)**, **lemon juice (2 tsp)**, and **salt (¼ tsp)**.
2. Blend until completely smooth and silky with zero paneer grains remaining.
3. Add the **5 large eggs** one by one, pulsing briefly after each addition just until combined. _(Do not overblend once eggs are added)._

### 3. Bake & Monitor

1. Pour the smooth batter into the prepared parchment-lined pan.
2. Bake at **220°C for 30 to 35 minutes**.
3. Check for doneness: The top should be deep golden/caramelized brown, while the inner **2–3 inches** of the center must still wobble significantly when shaken.
   _**Knife test:** Insert a knife straight into the center. If it comes out clean, the cheesecake is done._


### 4. Cool & Set

1. Remove from the oven and let it sit at room temperature for **2 hours** to sink, cool, and release residual heat.
2. Transfer to the refrigerator for **at least 5–6 hours** _(ideally overnight)_ so the core sets into a rich, creamy custard texture.

### 5. Prepare the Protein Chocolate Sauce (Topping)

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **chocolate whey protein powder**, **unsweetened cocoa powder**, and **low-fat cream/yogurt**.
2. Slowly add **milk** 1 tablespoon at a time while whisking vigorously until it turns into a glossy, smooth, saucy consistency.
3. Drizzle generously over your chilled slice right before serving for extra macros!
