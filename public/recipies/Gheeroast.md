## Prep & Prep Notes

### 1. The Chili Soak

Deseed the Byadgi and normal red chillies, then drop them into a bowl of hot water to soften up.

> **Pro-Tip for Paneer:** If you are using **high-fat (malai) paneer**, add the paneer cubes directly into the hot water bowl alongside the chillies. This makes the paneer exceptionally soft! Normal paneer can be used directly without soaking.

### 2. Chicken Marinade (If using Chicken)

If you are making **Chicken Ghee Roast** (works with both boneless and bone-in):

1. Whisk the dahi (curd) until smooth.
2. Marinate the chicken with a little bit of **Turmeric (Haldi)**, whisked dahi, and salt.
3. Let it rest while you prepare the paste.
4. Heat a pan, add a bit of ghee, and stir-fry the chicken until it is **~70% cooked**. Set it aside.

---

## Step-by-Step Cooking Guide

### Step 1: Grind the Signature Red Paste

Take the softened chillies out of the hot water and add them to a blender/grinder along with:

- 5–6 garlic cloves
- Cumin powder & black pepper
- Salt & jaggery
- Tamarind
- A tiny splash of water
- A fresh squeeze of lemon juice

Grind everything together into a smooth, thick, fine paste.

### Step 2: The Ghee Roast Base

1. Place a heavy-bottomed pan on medium heat and add **ghee as per your liking** (be generous!).
2. Throw in a handful of fresh **curry leaves** and let them crackle.
3. Pour in the freshly ground red paste.
4. Cook the paste on medium flame, stirring continuously, until it darkens and **nicely releases its oils** from the sides.

### Step 3: Combine & Simmer

1. Once the paste is shiny and releasing ghee, add your **paneer cubes** OR the **70% cooked chicken**.
2. Toss well to coat every piece in the thick masala.
3. Cover with a lid and let it simmer on **low-to-medium flame** for a few minutes so the flavors penetrate deep inside.
4. Remove the lid, give it a final stir, and serve hot! :prayge:
